How many illicit Bitcoin payments can a screening model catch?

At a glance
  1. 1 · WhatA Bitcoin fraud detector: graph and tabular models score transactions, with a dashboard, an AI assistant and risk analytics.
  2. 2 · WhyIllicit payments hide in a 200,000-transaction graph; investigators need a ranked, explained shortlist instead of raw flows.
  3. 3 · WhoUsers: anti-money-laundering analysts and investigators.Data: the public Elliptic dataset, 203,769 transactions; 4,545 labelled illicit and 42,019 licit (Weber et al. 2019).
  4. 4 · WhereResults page, technical walkthrough and transaction demo on GitHub Pages; dashboard on Streamlit Community Cloud; the API runs locally.
  5. 5 · When it failsChecked against the published benchmark first (illicit F1 within ±0.05 of 0.788): passed. A label inversion was found and fixed on the way.Every model collapses after a dark-market shutdown at time step 43 (F1 0.904 before, 0.031 after). No live A/B test: historical data.
  6. 6 · HowRandom forest on 166 features with the dataset authors' time split; GraphSAGE and GAT on the graph; a bandit picks the alert threshold.Result: illicit F1 0.815 on later time steps (published 0.788); F1 is the balance of catching fraud and avoiding false alarms (1 is perfect).
